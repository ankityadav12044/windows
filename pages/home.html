About Shyama & company

Brikkho Foundation is a community-led social welfare NGO dedicated to addressing essential needs across vulnerable communities in India. Our work focuses on providing nutritious meals to underprivileged children, promoting environmental sustainability through tree plantation initiatives, improving public hygiene through cleanliness drives, supporting dignity-based access to food, protecting bird habitats, and providing essential clothing support. By collaborating with local communities, volunteers, and supporters, we aim to create long-term, responsible, and inclusive social impact.

Who We Are

Brikkho Foundation is a community-led social welfare NGO committed to addressing essential human and environmental needs at the grassroots level. Our initiatives focus on nutrition for underprivileged children, environmental conservation through tree plantation, hygiene and cleanliness awareness, dignified access to food, protection of bird habitats, and essential clothing support. By working closely with local communities, volunteers, and supporters, we aim to create responsible, inclusive, and long-term social impact across India.
